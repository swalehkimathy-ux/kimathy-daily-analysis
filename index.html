Selection logic

The automatic builder follows the supplied accumulator rules and favors the highest-ranked eligible candidates first.

1
Sport-specific eligibilityFootball and basketball candidates use 1.05–1.80 per leg; only market-specific statistical evidence passes.
2
Highest score firstPending eligible fixtures are sorted by analysis score in descending order.
3
Stop at the targetAdd picks until combined odds reach at least 1.50, and never exceed 1.90.
4
Skip an overshootA leg that would push the ticket to 1.90 or higher is skipped while checking remaining candidates.
About the score: the 0–100 score is a heuristic ranking signal based on supplied criteria. It is not a calibrated probability, a guarantee, or a measure of expected profit.
